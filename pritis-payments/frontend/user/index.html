How it works

For reps

  1. Get verified. Apply as your class's rep with proof; Pritis checks it.
  2. Create a due. Set the amount and deadline. Pritis approves it before it goes live.
  3. Share one link. Post it in the class group. Students join and pay.
  4. Watch it fill up. See who has paid, download the list as PDF or CSV, and request a payout to your bank account.

For students

  1. Open your rep's link. Create an account once; it works for every due.
  2. Pay in a minute. Card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. You see one total, no surprises.
  3. Get your receipt. It's emailed to you straight away, and your payment shows on your dashboard.

Built so the money is always accounted for

  • Verified reps only. Every rep and every due is checked by Pritis before students can pay.
  • Secure payments. Paystack handles the payment; we never see your card details.
  • Every naira recorded. Each payment and payout is kept in a ledger that can't be edited.
  • Receipts for everyone. Students get a receipt by email; reps get a live paid list.
