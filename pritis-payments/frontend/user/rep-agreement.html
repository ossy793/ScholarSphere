Pritis DPP Student Terms

Rep Services Agreement

Version 1.0 · effective 20 October 2026

This Agreement is between Pritis, powered by Corax Limited ("Pritis", "we", "us"), and the elected class representative named in the Rep application ("the Rep", "you"), acting on behalf of the students of the class named in that application ("the class").

You accept this Agreement by ticking "I agree" when you apply to be a Rep, or by using Pritis DPP to collect money for your class. Pritis accepts it by verifying you as the Rep. The record of your acceptance (date and time) is kept with your application.

1. Your role

  • You act for the class, not for yourself. You must be the class's genuine elected representative, and you must give true information and a genuine proof of studentship when you apply.
  • You set each due's title, amount and deadline. Pritis checks every due, and every change to it, before students can pay.
  • You must use the money collected only for the purpose you gave for the due, and be ready to account to your class for it.
  • No personal liability for unpaid students. You aren't personally or financially responsible to Pritis if some students don't pay.

2. Fees

Pritis DPP has no sign-up fee, subscription, maintenance fee, per-student charge or automatic renewal. There are only two charges:

  • Student service charge: added on top of each due and paid by the student, so it doesn't come out of the amount you set. It's currently 4.5% of the due (1.5% for payment processing and 3% Pritis platform fee). You see the exact total each student pays when you create the due.
  • Payout charge: when you withdraw collected money to your bank account, 1% of the amount plus Paystack's transfer fee (currently ₦10, ₦25 or ₦50, depending on the amount). You see the exact charge before you confirm each payout.

We'll give you at least 14 days' notice by email before changing any fee.

3. Collecting and receiving money

  • Students pay through Paystack. Each payment shows in your balance as Pending, and becomes Available once the funds have cleared (normally within 24 hours).
  • You can request a payout of your Available balance at any time. Payouts go only to the bank account you registered and we verified. Changing that account needs our approval, and payouts pause while we check it.
  • If a student is refunded, their share comes off your balance. If you've already been paid that money, the amount is taken from your next collections before your next payout.
  • You can see in real time who has paid and who hasn't, and download each due's paid list as PDF or CSV at any time.

4. Student privacy and data protection

  • We comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and collect only the minimum data needed to run the service: students' names, emails, phone numbers, university, level, matric numbers (when a due asks for them), login details and payment records. Card and bank details are handled by Paystack; we never see or store them.
  • We never sell, lease or share student data with advertisers, data brokers or anyone for marketing. We share it only with the providers that run the service (payments, email, hosting and secure storage), for that purpose.
  • You'll see your class's names, matric numbers and payment status. Use them only for managing your class's dues, keep them private, and don't share them outside the class.

5. Service standards and support

  • Availability: we commit to Pritis DPP being available at least 99.5% of each month. Planned maintenance doesn't count; we announce it at least 24 hours ahead and never schedule it in the last 48 hours before one of your deadlines.
  • Devices: Pritis DPP works on phones (Android and iPhone) and on computers.
  • Reporting problems: report a bug in writing through Contact support (or WhatsApp, followed by a support message), so there's a record.
  • Urgent fixes: for a critical problem that stops students paying or you collecting, we acknowledge your report and deploy a fix or a working alternative within 24 hours of your written report.

6. If Pritis DPP is down while you're collecting

If Pritis DPP is unavailable for more than 24 hours in a row while one of your dues is open for payment, then on request:

  • we extend that due's deadline free of charge; and
  • we pay out the money already collected for that due without the payout charge, within 7 business days of your request.

Ask through Contact support within 14 days of the outage. This is the remedy for downtime under this Agreement.

7. Handing over to the next Rep

  • When your class elects a new Rep, the outgoing Rep (or the class) tells us through Contact support. Once the new Rep is verified, we move the class, its dues and payment history to them at no cost, with no new agreement to negotiate.
  • Money not yet paid out stays with the class: before the handover, the outgoing Rep either pays it out for the class's purposes or it moves to the new Rep, as the class agrees in writing.

8. When the class leaves or graduates

  • Your data: on request, within 14 days we send the Rep a structured export (CSV) of the class's members and payment records.
  • Deletion: within 14 days of a successful handover of that export, we delete the students' accounts and personal data from our active systems. We keep only the payment records Nigerian tax and company law requires (generally six years), with access restricted. Deleted data leaves our encrypted backups as they expire, within 90 days.

9. Suspension

We may pause a Rep's dues and payouts while we investigate suspected fraud, false information or misuse of class money. We'll tell you why, and protect the students' money while we do.

10. General

  • As far as the law allows, our total responsibility under this Agreement is limited to the fees Pritis received from your class's dues in the 12 months before a claim.
  • We'll email you at least 14 days before changing this Agreement. If you keep using Pritis DPP after that, the new version applies.
  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
  • Contact: Contact support on the site, or WhatsApp 0903 283 3347.
